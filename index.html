Rajma Chawal + Curd Recipe

Hostel & Student Friendly Quick Prep (Under 20 Mins)

Ingredients:

1 cup soaked red kidney beans (rajma), 1 cup basmati rice, 1 onion, 1 tomato, ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp ghee, turmeric, cumin, and coriander powder.

Step-by-Step Instructions:
  1. Pressure cook soaked rajma with salt and turmeric for 5-6 whistles.
  2. In a pan, sauté onions, ginger-garlic paste, and tomatoes with spices until oil separates.
  3. Mix cooked rajma into the masala gravy and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Serve hot with steamed basmati rice and a side bowl of fresh curd.
